About Me

The analyst behind the dashboards

I'm a data analyst who specializes in helping e-commerce owners understand what their data is actually saying.

Most store owners sit on a goldmine of data! Sales records, customer behavior, ad spend, inventory logs but rarely know what to do with it. That's where I come in. I take the messy, scattered data across your Shopify, WooCommerce, or Amazon store and turn it into clear, visual insights that tell you exactly what to do next.

Whether it's identifying which products are secretly eating your margins, which customer segments are worth 10x more than others, or which marketing channels are burning cash — I make sure you know, with data to back it up.

My Process

How I work with e-commerce brands

01

Discovery Call

We discuss your store, your goals, and the data questions keeping you up at night. I map out what data you have and what we need.

02

Data Audit & Cleanup

I connect to your data sources, clean the mess, and build a reliable foundation. No insights are useful if the data is broken.

03

Analysis & Insights

Deep-dive into your numbers using SQL, Python, and BI tools. I find the patterns, anomalies, and opportunities hiding in your data.

04

Deliver & Act

You get a clear report with visualizations, plain-English recommendations, and a live dashboard. We review together and plan next steps.
